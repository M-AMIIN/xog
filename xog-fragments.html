History Arrives in Pieces

This is where the archive does its work. A piece of evidence enters as a fragment with its provenance. When a second independent source supports the same claim, an editor files them together as a thread and writes the hypothesis. At three independent sources the entry is established and moves to the Sources page. Contradictions are filed as disputes and stay visible.

XOG-EST-001 Punt traded with Egypt from the Somali coast
Ancient · Trade · woven from 3 sources
 Established

Filed as established. Three independent lines of evidence support the claim, and the entry now lives on the Punt source page. The chain remains here so the weave can be re-walked.

Source 1 · iconographic — Deir el-Bahri reliefs, c. 1470 BCE: goods, stilt houses, fauna, coastline.
Source 2 · textual — Egyptian records across two millennia: Sahure, Hatshepsut, Ramesses III. Trade in every documented contact.
Source 3 · physical — isotope analysis of mummified baboons (Dominy et al., 2020) tracing origins to the Horn corridor.
Filed by: this archive · status reached: 2026
XOG-DSP-001 The exact location of Punt's core territory
Ancient · two readings on the board
 Disputed

Established that Punt traded from the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden world; disputed where its core stood. The archive's map draws both readings rather than choosing quietly.

Reading A — African core: the Horn coast and hinterland, supported by the relief landscape and the baboon isotopes.
Reading B — a reach extending to the Arabian shore, argued from trade-goods distribution.
Sought — excavation evidence from either shore naming Puntite settlement.

XOG-THR-004 Continuous monetised trade across the western Indian Ocean
Trade · Ajuran · 2 of 3 sources
 Thread · 2/3

Hypothesis: the Somali coast ran continuous monetised trade from antiquity through the Ajuran period. Two independent sources twist; the third that would weave it is named below.

Source 1 · textual — the Periplus, 1st c. CE: the same coast exporting port by port.
Source 2 · numismatic — Benadir-minted coins reported from Mozambique to Sri Lanka.
Sought — a catalogued excavation report placing Benadir coinage in a dated stratum.
Filed by: this archive · hypothesis written: 2026
XOG-FRG-002 The missing second half of the Futuh al-Habasha
Adal · Manuscripts · 1 source
 Fragment

The surviving Futuh ends at 1537, six years before the campaign did. Burton reported hearing that the remainder existed in Mocha or Hudaydah. One source, held as a fragment.

Source 1 · report — Richard Burton, 19th c., unverified hearsay of a Yemeni copy.
Sought — any entry in a Yemeni manuscript catalogue; any traveller's inventory from Mocha or Hudaydah.
Filed by: this archive · the find of the century if it weaves
XOG-FRG-003 The full regional name-lists of the 28 stations
Astronomy · Oral knowledge · 1 source
 Fragment

Galaal recorded that station names varied across the seven weather districts and preserved only part of the set. The remainder may live in elders' memories now. Each recorded name, with district and reciter, files as its own fragment.

Source 1 · ethnographic — Galaal 1968, partial lists, seven districts named.
Sought — station names recorded from a named reciter, with district.
