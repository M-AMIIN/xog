Black Hawk Down: The Somali Account

OCTOBER 3, 1993 · MOGADISHU · SOURCES IN COMPILATION

The international record of October 3, 1993 consists of three sources: American military memoirs, Mark Bowden’s journalistic reconstruction, and Ridley Scott’s film. All three describe the same eighteen hours from the same side. The American soldiers are named. Their families are interviewed. Their fear is rendered in detail. The Somali side of the street is a faceless crowd.

Between 300 and 1,000 Somalis died on October 3 and 4. The precise number has never been established because no one conducted a count. The American dead — eighteen — are named on a memorial. The Somali dead are a range estimate.

Xog is compiling the Somali account. Testimonies from families who lived in Bakara Market. Accounts from militia fighters who participated in the battle. Interviews with clan elders who negotiated the retrieval of bodies and the eventual American withdrawal. Medical records from Digfer Hospital and Medina Hospital, which received casualties throughout the night.

This project is not an argument that the American account is wrong. It is a compilation of the account that was never assembled. When both accounts exist side by side, the reader can see what was visible from each position — and what was not.

This source is in active compilation. Publication date pending.
